    Frequently asked questions

    Is my text sent anywhere?

    No. The cleanup engine is plain JavaScript that runs in your browser. Your draft is processed on your device and is not sent to or stored on a server.

    What exactly does it change?

    Em dashes (replaced with a comma, period or colon based on context), robotic intros and sign-offs (“Certainly!”, “Great question!”, “I hope this helps!”), stock openers like “In today's fast-paced world”, the “It's not just X, it's Y” pattern, and overused words such as delve, testament, tapestry, game-changer, leverage, unlock and landscape. After each cleanup you get a list of what changed.

    Will it rewrite my whole post?

    No. It makes targeted, rule-based edits and leaves the rest of your wording alone. Give the result a quick read before you post.

    Does it guarantee my post passes AI detectors?

    No. CleanDraft removes the most common tells that readers notice. It doesn't promise any detector score, and the “AI tells” number on this page is just a count of what the engine found.

    How do the free cleanups work?

    You get 10 free cleanups, counted in this browser. After that, CleanDraft Unlimited is $9/month.
